Trump says Ukraine should 'get a new president' after Zelensky condemns Russia diesel deal

Trump announced Friday that Russia would supply more than 300,000 tons of diesel to US and global markets, with sanctions on Russian fuel temporarily eased. Zelensky, surprised by the deal, called it "not fair and not honest" and a "birthday gift" to Putin. On Saturday Trump said it was "time for Ukraine to get a new president" who can make a deal, and demanded Kyiv stop striking Russian refineries. Axios called it the most serious crisis between the two leaders since their February 2025 blowup.

It signals a sharp shift in US policy on Ukraine and on sanctions against Russia.
